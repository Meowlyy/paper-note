Chapter One — The Departure

I'll be gone for a while...

hey, it's me :) if you found this, i'm probably already gone for a bit. take your time with this, it's for you.

i don't know how long. could be a few weeks, could be longer, i honestly don't know yet. i'm stepping away from Discord, Twitch, all of it — the online stuff, for real this time. not because of you. i just need this.

Chapter Two — The Real Struggle

Feeling alone, even when I'm not

truth is, i'm not doing so good right now. it's not one big thing, it's just... everything, kind of at once. Discord, Twitch, being reachable all the time — it stopped feeling good a while ago, and i need actual distance from it, not just muting notifications for a night.

i've been feeling alone a lot lately. even mid-call with people, even in a group chat blowing up — alone. and i know that's not the whole truth. i've got people who stand behind me, starting with my family, but it doesn't stop there. saying i have no one wouldn't be fair to them, or to anyone who's actually shown up for me.

if that's you, in any way, big or small — you have a place in my heart. i mean that more than i can really put into words. thank you, all of you <3

Chapter Three — Scars, Pain & Not Giving Up

Still standing

i carry some things. bad experiences, people who hurt me, traumas i don't really talk about out loud. that's still part of me, whether i like it or not, and i'm done pretending it isn't.

but i'm not giving up. i'll keep trying my best, no matter how much it hurts. and if i end up crying at some point because of everything sitting inside me — that's okay too. it doesn't mean i'm losing.

i'm going to try to hold onto my smile as much as i can. i want to stay the person i actually am underneath all of this — good-hearted, even when it's hard. especially when it's hard.

Chapter Four — Final Words

A place in my heart

i might still go through hard times after this. i might make decisions i don't fully understand yet myself. but i'm trying not to regret them.

and even if i don't come back the same — not fully, or not in the way you'd expect — i won't forget you. any of you.

there's a part of me that's always going to have room for the people who were here. that doesn't go away, wherever i end up.
